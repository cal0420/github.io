Official policy

PRIVACY

Last updated: 29 July 2026

This policy covers C4G Bot, C4G Music Bot 1, C4G Music Bot 2 and C4G Music Bot 3.

Information processed

The applications may process Discord user, server, channel, role and message identifiers; usernames and display names; command inputs; music searches and queue information; levels, balances and inventories; moderation records; temporary voice settings; server configuration; and technical logs.

How information is used

Information is used to provide requested bot features, preserve settings, operate queues, manage moderation and safety tools, diagnose faults and prevent abuse.

Sharing

Personal information is not sold. Information may be processed by Discord, hosting providers and services required to operate the applications, or disclosed where legally required.

Deletion requests

Users and server administrators may request deletion of associated stored information by contacting Cal420 on Discord and providing the relevant user or server ID.

Security and changes

Reasonable safeguards are used, although no internet service can guarantee absolute security. This policy may be updated as the project changes.
