Questions

Premium FAQ

These plans are a preview of the direction C4G Premium may take.

Can I subscribe today?

Not yet. C4G Premium remains in development, so no payments are being accepted.

Will free features be removed?

No. The free tier is intended to keep the core bot experience available. Premium adds capacity, customisation and advanced tools.

When will Premium launch?

Premium is planned for after the bot ecosystem reaches a stable public release.

Are these final prices?

No. The listed prices and features are previews and may be adjusted before subscriptions become available.
